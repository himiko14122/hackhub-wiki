The HackHub Journalist Sister mission sits in a weird spot on most players' to-do lists. It looks like a side errand when you pick it up, but the rewards and the dialogue rewards push it well above the average contract. Get the full picture before you load in.

## Journalist Sister Mission Overview and Unlock Requirements

The Journalist Sister mission drops you into a mid-game neighborhood hub that doubles as a hostile data brokerage. You are not there to clear a map. You are there to extract a single NPC, protect her while she runs a broadcast, and keep her alive long enough to deliver an evidence package. That sounds simple on paper, but the map is crawling with patrol drones, two scripted ambush waves, and a sniper who scopes the rooftops once the alarm triggers.

Players pick up the contract from the **Mission Board** in the South Quarter after completing the previous chapter's main story arc. According to community data, the unlock flag only flips once you finish the preceding side chain called **Frequency Bleed**, so runners who try to grab it early will see a locked prompt and no quest marker.

### How to Unlock the Mission

You need three things before the Journalist Sister mission becomes available:

- Finish the **Frequency Bleed** side chain, which itself requires reaching **Chapter 4**.
- Reach **Reputation Tier 3** with the Civic Press faction so the journalist trusts you enough to make contact.
- Own at least one stealth-capable gadget (a suppressed sidearm, a hacking knife, or a cloaking tag) since the mission rating is **stealth-friendly** but not stealth-locked.

Once those conditions are met, head to the South Quarter station and look for the **Press Liaison** terminal. The mission entry costs **0 credits** to accept, but it charges a **150-credit deposit** that is refunded on completion, even if you choose a loud route.

### Mission Rating and Difficulty Tags

The contract uses a layered rating system that tells you what kind of loadout the designers expect. Here is the breakdown community runners have mapped out.

| Tag | Value | What It Means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Threat Level | 3 / 5 | Mid-tier enemy density |
| Stealth Score | High | Bonus payout for no detections |
| Time Pressure | Moderate | 18-minute soft timer, hard fail at 30 |
| Squad Size | 1-2 | Solo or one NPC partner supported |
| Boss Checkpoint | Yes | Wave-2 ambush acts as a soft boss |

The soft timer is what trips most first-time runners. You have a comfortable 18-minute window to finish the core objectives, but if you trigger the second wave too early, the **Hard Fail** clock kicks in and the mission ends regardless of progress. Plan your route so you reach the broadcast booth with at least **5 minutes** left on the clock.

## Journalist Sister Mission Objectives and Full Walkthrough

The mission pushes you through a single dense district with three primary objectives, two optional objectives, and a hidden bonus that only triggers if you keep the journalist alive through every scripted encounter. Below is the full step list community runners have consolidated, along with tips for each phase.

### Primary Objectives

There are three non-skippable objectives that gate the mission completion reward. The order shown below is the most efficient path according to community testing.

| # | Objective | Location | Approx. Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Reach the **Rooftop Rendezvous** | North tower, top floor | 2-3 min |
| 2 | Escort **Naomi Kerr** to the **Broadcast Booth** | Central plaza | 6-8 min |
| 3 | Defend the booth through **Wave 2** | Central plaza | 5-7 min |

Each objective is gated by a checkpoint. If you go down between objectives 2 and 3, you respawn at the broadcast booth entrance with full ammo and the journalist's trust intact, which makes second attempts much less painful than restarting from zero.

### Optional Objectives

You can stack the mission payout with two optional objectives. The first is optional because it requires a specific gadget, and the second because it pulls you off the optimal route.

- **Tag the Drone Patrol Routes** — install a **Tracking Beacon** on three patrol drones. Rewards **+250 credits** and the **Eyes On You** achievement.
- **Recover the Press Credentials** — a small loot box hidden in the basement of the south office. Rewards the **Press Pass** key item, which unlocks a discount vendor in the South Quarter hub.

The tracking beacon route adds about **4 minutes** to your run but pays for the mission deposit twice over if you plan to farm the area. The press credentials are a quality-of-life pickup rather than a raw payout, so newer runners can skip it without losing combat value.

## Key NPCs, Enemies, and the Journalist Sister Profile

The Journalist Sister mission is one of the few contracts where a named NPC fights alongside you and can actually go down permanently if you ignore her. Understanding her kit and the enemy roster is the difference between a clean clear and a restart.

### Meet Naomi Kerr, The Journalist Sister

Naomi Kerr is a mid-forties reporter who specializes in corporate corruption. In combat she carries a **light pistol**, **35 rounds** of reserve ammo, and a single **Flashbang Grenade** that she uses automatically when three or more enemies swarm her. She cannot take more than **two hits** before going into downed state, and once she is downed the mission soft-fails unless you revive her within **20 seconds**.

She follows at a fixed distance and mirrors your sprint, but she does not take cover automatically. According to community data, the safest pattern is to clear every room before she enters, because she will walk into crossfire if you push ahead. Veteran runners recommend assigning her a waypoint with the **Hold Position** command at chokepoints so she stays out of sniper sightlines.

### Enemy Types and Patrol Behavior

You will face five distinct enemy archetypes inside the mission district. Below is the full combat reference community testers have published.

| Enemy | Role | Detection Range | Weakness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Light Drone** | Patrol, scout | Short (8m) | EMP or melee |
| **Heavy Bruiser** | Wave 2 melee tank | None, aggro on sight | Headshot, fire |
| **Rifleman** | Mid-range support | Medium (15m) | Suppressed flanks |
| **Sniper** | Rooftop overwatch | Long (30m) | Hack terminal or cloaking tag |
| **Saboteur** | Disables broadcast | Melee only | Stun or one-shot |

The sniper is the priority target once Wave 2 begins. Two snipers spawn on the rooftops flanking the broadcast booth, and they will one-shot Naomi within two seconds of the alarm triggering. Deal with them first using your **Hacking Knife** on the rooftop terminals, which tags both snipers as friendly to your cloak.

## Rewards, Loot Table, and Best Loadout for the Mission

The reward package for completing the HackHub Journalist Sister mission is one of the more generous payouts in the mid-game. You get paid for the main clear, for each optional objective, and for keeping Naomi alive, which stacks multiplicatively if you finish the run without a single detection.

### Reward Breakdown

| Completion Tier | Credits | Bonus Items |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main Clear Only | 1,200 | **Encrypted Datachip** |
| + One Optional | 1,650 | Datachip + **Press Token** |
| + Both Optional | 2,200 | Datachip + Press Token + **Naomi's Recorder** |
| Perfect Run (no detection, Naomi alive) | 3,000 | All of the above + **Sister's Badge** cosmetic |

The **Sister's Badge** is the long-tail chase. It is a shoulder-mounted cosmetic that unlocks a unique dialogue option with the Civic Press vendor, who then sells you a discounted **Press Drone** deployable. If you only care about raw credits, the **Perfect Run** tier is roughly **2.5x** the value of a basic clear, so the stealth investment pays off even if you are a combat-first runner.

### Recommended Loadout

Community-tested loadouts for this mission cluster around two builds. The stealth build is faster but riskier if you miss a patrol, while the combat build is slower but forgives mistakes.

- **Stealth Build:** Suppressed sidearm, Hacking Knife x2, Cloaking Tag x3, Light Drone hack module, Med Kit x1.
- **Combat Build:** Assault rifle with reflex sight, Stun Grenades x4, Heavy Plate, Med Kit x2, Flashbang.
- **Hybrid Build:** Suppressed SMG, one Hacking Knife, two Cloaking Tags, Med Kit x2, Flashbang x2.

The hybrid build is the sweet spot for most runners because it lets you clear the early patrol phase quietly and still answers the Wave 2 bruisers with raw damage. Pair it with the **Casting Distance** upgrade if you have it, since longer cloak windows let you escort Naomi through open plaza sections without burning your entire bar.

## Strategy, Stealth Route, and Combat Tips for the HackHub Journalist Sister Mission

A clean run of the Journalist Sister mission takes about **12 minutes** if you plan the route well. A messy run can drag past **25 minutes** and still fail at the hard timer. The difference is sequencing, not raw skill, so spend a minute planning before you drop in.

### Stealth Route Through the Mission

The optimal stealth route enters from the **north alley**, picks up the **Rooftop Rendezvous** first, then cuts through the maintenance corridor to the broadcast booth. This path avoids every scripted patrol except one, and the single patrol can be bypassed with a well-timed cloak if you have the **Casting Distance** upgrade maxed.

Key tricks community runners rely on:

- Use the **Tracking Beacon** on the first drone you see, because it lets you see the entire patrol network on your minimap.
- Drop Naomi at the **Maintenance Corridor** junction and tell her to hold while you clear the sniper rooftop first.
- Do not pick up the **Press Credentials** until after Wave 2, because the basement path crosses two aggro zones that re-arm during the defense phase.
- Save your last **Cloaking Tag** for the moment Naomi crosses the plaza, since that is the highest-risk segment of the run.

### Combat Route Through the Mission

If you prefer loud clears, the combat route is more forgiving but slower. Rush the broadcast booth first, fortify the doorway with **two stun grenades** pre-staged, then clear the rooftops before triggering Wave 2. The snipers are the only true lethal threat, so burn a **Hacking Knife** on the rooftop terminal the moment you reach the booth.

Combat runners should bring at least **two Med Kits** because Wave 2 includes a **Saboteur** enemy that rushes the broadcast equipment. If the Saboteur reaches the console before you down it, the mission fails the **defend the booth** objective and you lose about **800 credits** of reward value. The safest counter is to keep a **Flashbang** equipped and cycle it the moment you hear the alert ping.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What level should I be before attempting the HackHub Journalist Sister mission?**

Most community runners recommend **Chapter 4**, gear score around **420**, and at least one stealth gadget unlocked. Going in undergeared will not lock the mission, but the Wave 2 bruisers will melt you in two hits if your armor is below that threshold.

**Can I complete the Journalist Sister mission solo without stealth gadgets?**

Yes. A loud combat clear is fully supported, though it takes longer and skips the Perfect Run bonus. Expect around **20 minutes** for a loud clear versus **12 minutes** for a stealth clear, and bring extra Med Kits because Naomi will take more incidental damage.

**Does Naomi die permanently if she is downed?**

No. Naomi enters a downed state when she loses all her health, and you have a **20-second revive window**. If you fail to revive her in time, the mission soft-fails and you restart from the broadcast booth checkpoint, but she is restored to full health on retry.

**What is the fastest way to get the Sister's Badge cosmetic?**

Stack the **Perfect Run** conditions: finish every primary objective, complete both optional objectives, keep Naomi alive, and never trigger a detection alert. The badge only drops when all four conditions are met in a single attempt, so do not split the run across sessions.

**Can I replay the HackHub Journalist Sister mission for farming?**

Yes. The mission has a **15-minute cooldown** between attempts, and the reward tier scales with your current reputation. At **Reputation Tier 5** the Perfect Run payout climbs to roughly **3,400 credits**, which makes it one of the better mid-game farms once you have the route memorized.
