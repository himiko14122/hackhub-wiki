## Understanding the Mission Board and Objective Types

Before diving into specific paths, it is essential to understand how the **mission board** categorizes tasks because the objective type determines whether you should build for speed, stealth, or combat endurance. The board refreshes every four hours, and while some missions are simple fetch quests, others require you to navigate multi-stage encounters where a single wrong dialogue choice can lock you out of the final reward. According to community data, players who filter missions by **reward tier** rather than difficulty rating complete their collection goals up to 40% faster than those who accept the first available contract.

### Core Mission Categories and Payouts

Each mission falls into one of five structural types, and recognizing the pattern before you deploy can save you from wasting consumables on a run that does not match your current loadout. The table below breaks down the most common categories you will encounter on the objective board.

| Mission Category | Primary Objective | Typical Payout Range | Risk Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Data Retrieval** | Extract intel from secure nodes | 800-1,200 credits | Low |
| **Asset Protection** | Defend a terminal or convoy | 1,000-1,800 credits | Medium |
| **Target Neutralization** | Eliminate high-value NPCs | 1,500-2,500 credits | High |
| **Stealth Infiltration** | Avoid detection while hacking | 1,200-2,000 credits | High |

The **Stealth Infiltration** missions are frequently cited by the community as the most punishing, primarily because detection not only fails the mission but also triggers a fifteen-minute debuff that reduces your next mission’s credit payout by 20%.

### Reading the Objective List for Hidden Conditions

A common mistake is skimming the primary objective without expanding the secondary conditions, which often contain the actual failure triggers. When you accept a mission, you should always check for three hidden parameters: **time limits**, **equipment restrictions**, and **lethality clauses**. For example, some **Target Neutralization** contracts require a non-lethal approach despite the mission name suggesting otherwise, and using lethal force will immediately fail the objective and mark the contract as void. Before you deploy, confirm that your equipped cyberdeck and weapon mods match the fine-print restrictions, because bringing a restricted item will lock you out of the mission start point without a refund.

## Advanced Strategies for Hard-Mode Mission Variants

Hard-mode variants of standard missions introduce a **corruption meter** that fills whenever you take damage or fail a hacking attempt, and if this meter reaches 100%, the mission fails instantly regardless of your remaining objectives. These variants pay out nearly double the standard credit reward, but the corruption mechanic forces you to play flawlessly or abandon the run early to save your consumables. According to player data, the most consistent way to manage the **corruption meter** is to bring a **Cleanse Protocol** consumable and to avoid any optional combat encounters that are not required to reach the objective node.

### When to Abandon a Hard-Mode Run

Knowing when to cut your losses is a critical skill for hard-mode missions, because a failed run not only wastes your consumables but also triggers a longer cooldown than a voluntary extraction. If your corruption meter exceeds 60% before you reach the second objective, the probability of completing the mission drops below 20%, and you should extract immediately to preserve your equipped modules. The credits you lose from an early extraction are always less than the cost of replacing a destroyed Mk.IV module.

Most failures result from hidden secondary conditions like **non-lethal requirements** or **time limits** that are listed in the fine print of the mission contract. Always expand the objective details before deploying to check for equipment restrictions and lethality clauses.

**How do I avoid the extraction timer bug?**

The extraction timer starts invisibly the moment you pick up the objective item, and a known bug sometimes causes the timer to begin before you reach the item if you trigger a zone transition. To avoid this, do not enter any elevators or doors that trigger a loading screen after the objective item is within your pickup radius.

**Can I replay missions for the same rewards?**

Yes, missions refresh on a four-hour cooldown cycle, and you can replay the same contract type for the same credit payout, but rare blueprint drops are limited to the first three completions per week. After that, the drop rate decreases significantly according to community testing.

**What is the best loadout for stealth infiltration missions?**

A Mk.IV **Cypher Decoder** paired with a low-profile chassis and a silenced weapon mod is the most consistent loadout for stealth missions, because it maximizes your decryption speed while minimizing your detection radius. Avoid any equipment that increases your heat signature, which draws enemy patrols toward your position.
