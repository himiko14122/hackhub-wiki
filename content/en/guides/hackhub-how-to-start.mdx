## Understanding Your First Terminal
The game begins with a barebones terminal that is intentionally slow to challenge your patience and resource management skills. You are not meant to keep the default rig for long, but you need to know exactly what you are working with before you start spending your hard-earned cash. The **HackHub how to start** journey always begins by assessing the **Processing Speed** and **RAM Capacity** of your current setup, because launching jobs with insufficient memory will cause immediate failure and force you to forfeit a contract deposit.

The starter terminal typically comes with a **Single-Core Processor** and **256MB of RAM**, which is barely enough to run the simplest intrusion scripts. To avoid getting stuck in a loop of failed jobs, you should immediately look at the **Job Board** and filter tasks by the minimal hardware requirements. Many new players skip this filter and find themselves staring at a red error screen because their rig can't handle the **ICE Breaker** software demands.

### Job Filtering Mechanics
Job filtering is not just a quality-of-life feature; it is a survival mechanism in the early game. The board refreshes with new contracts every **5 minutes** in real-time, but you can manually refresh it for a small fee. When considering **HackHub how to start** efficiently, you need to ignore the payout amount at first and focus exclusively on hardware compatibility.

The following table breaks down the basic job tiers in the early game and what they require from your rig:

| Job Tier | Minimum RAM (MB) | Recommended Processor | Risk Level | New-Player Friendly |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Data Sweep** | 128 | Single-Core | Low | Yes |
| **Ping Spoof** | 256 | Single-Core | Low | Yes |
| **Port Scan** | 512 | Dual-Core | Medium | No |
| **Password Crack** | 1024 | Dual-Core | High | No |

Sticking to **Data Sweep** and **Ping Spoof** contracts while you save for the **Dual-Core Processor** is the safest route, because failing a contract with a high **Risk Level** not only wastes time but also increases your **Trace Probability** meter, which can lead to temporary bans from the network.

## Optimizing Trace Management
Trace management is the most punishing mechanic for players who don't research **HackHub how to start** guides. Every action you take builds up a hidden **Trace Probability** meter, and when it hits 100%, your session is compromised. The meter decays slowly in real-time, but you can accelerate this by logging off for a set period.

The **Trace Probability** meter is not just a timer; it's a dynamic risk assessment that scales with the difficulty of your active scripts. Running a **Port Scan** while simultaneously downloading a database will spike the meter twice as fast. To counter this, you need to install a **Logic Bomb** script that masks your signal, but this requires the **Dual-Core Processor** to run alongside your primary tool.

If the **Data Sweep** finishes before the **Logic Bomb** has masked your signal, your trace meter will spike, because the game registers a raw, unmasked transaction. To avoid this, you must calculate the execution time of your tools. The **Data Sweep** has a base execution time of **45 seconds**, while the **Logic Bomb** requires **60 seconds** to deploy.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the first piece of hardware I should buy in HackHub?**

The **Dual-Core Processor** is the undisputed first purchase. It unlocks the ability to run two scripts at once and enables medium-tier jobs like **Port Scan**, which pays significantly more than the starter jobs. Skipping this to buy RAM first will result in a slower overall progression speed.

**How do I avoid getting traced so quickly?**

You need to monitor the **Trace Probability** meter and invest in a **Basic Firewall** immediately after your processor upgrade. Synchronize your scripts so that a **Logic Bomb** masks your signal while you extract data. If the meter hits 75%, stop all activity and let the meter cool down naturally.

**Can I make money while I am offline?**

Yes, but not directly. The **cooldown timer** for your trace meter continues to tick while you are offline, which is a critical part of the **HackHub how to start** loop. Logging off for 10-15 minutes resets your risk profile, allowing you to jump straight back into high-value contracts without waiting around in-game.

**Why do I keep failing Password Crack jobs?**

You are likely failing because your **RAM Capacity** is insufficient to handle the hash collision. **Password Crack** contracts require a minimum of **1024MB of RAM** to run without errors. If you attempt them with the starter 256MB stick, the script will crash 90% of the time, wasting your deposit.

**Is the GPU worth buying early?**

No. The GPU is a late-game luxury for visual decryption tasks. In the early stages of **HackHub how to start**, you should focus entirely on the **Dual-Core Processor** and **512MB RAM Stick**. Spending money on a GPU before you have a stable income will leave you stuck running low-tier jobs for much longer.
