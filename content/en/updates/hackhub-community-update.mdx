## Major Balance Changes and Stat Adjustments

Competitive balance sits at the heart of every HackHub community update, and patch 2.4.1 makes some of the largest swings the meta has seen in months. The development team explained in their developer notes that the goal was to curb the dominance of high-burst compositions without neutering the identity of any single class.

### Weapon and Combat Tuning

Seven weapons received numerical adjustments, with the bulk of changes targeting **damage values** and **cooldown reduction** windows. Below is the full breakdown reported by players who datamined the live client within hours of the patch going live.

| Weapon | Previous Damage | New Damage | Cooldown Change | Effective Tier |
|--------|----------------|------------|-----------------|----------------|
| Pulse Rifle | 28 per hit | 32 per hit | Unchanged | A |
| Shadow Blade | 45 crit | 50 crit | -1.5s | S |

The **Shadow Blade** emerges as the biggest winner in this HackHub community update, picking up both a damage bump and a shorter cooldown that lets aggressive players chain finishers more reliably. Conversely, the **Nova Cannon** loses ground because its area-of-effect radius was trimmed alongside the damage reduction, which community reports suggest makes it harder to one-shot clustered objectives.

### Character Ability Rebalances

Beyond weapons, four signature abilities were tuned to address what the studio called "stagger issues" in late-game encounters.

| Ability | Hero | Old Effect | New Effect | Reason |

Players running **Healing Aura** builds will notice the throughput increase matters most during the new raid encounter detailed later in this HackHub community update.

## New Features and Quality of Life Improvements

Outside of pure numbers, the HackHub community update brings a fresh batch of interface polish and behind-the-scenes upgrades that affect every match you queue into.

### UI and Inventory Updates

The inventory screen has been rebuilt from the ground up, replacing the legacy grid with a tabbed layout that separates weapons, mods, and consumables. The headline addition is the **auto-sort feature**, which organizes items by rarity, type, or recent acquisition depending on the filter you select. Community testers praised how this cut down mid-match fumbling, especially for newer players who previously hoarded materials without knowing what to use.

A secondary quality-of-life win is the **material filtering toggle** on the crafting bench. You can now hide items below a certain rarity, which streamlines the upgrade path when you are chasing a specific **mod drop**. The team also added stack counters that show total inventory weight at a glance, addressing complaints that surfaced during the previous patch cycle.

Every major HackHub community update pairs balance work with fresh activities to keep the player base engaged, and patch 2.4.1 is no exception. The headline addition is the **Phantom Protocol raid**, a three-week limited event that tasks squads with dismantling a fortified AI network across five escalating stages.

Here is what players can earn by completing the event:

- **Cipher Frame** weapon skin for the Pulse Rifle (unlocks at stage 3 clear)
- **Voidwalker** title and accompanying emote (stage 5 clear, all five classes)
- **Glitch Pup** pet cosmetic (random drop from final boss chest)
- 1,500 **event currency** redeemable in the rotating shop for past-season items
- Bonus **crafting materials** scaling with squad performance rating

The event introduces a new affix system where each stage layers a modifier like "shielded elites" or "low gravity," forcing teams to adapt their compositions on the fly. According to community data, top-tier guilds are already experimenting with double-Medic squads because the **Healing Aura** buff mentioned earlier makes sustained pushes more viable than burst strategies.

Beyond the raid, the developers also confirmed that the seasonal **battle pass** will receive a mid-season extension, adding ten new tiers with **cosmetic items** and a small chunk of premium currency. This addresses player feedback that recent passes felt front-loaded, and it gives returning players a reason to dive back in.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When did the latest HackHub community update go live?**

Patch 2.4.1 deployed on all platforms simultaneously following a brief maintenance window. According to community data, the rollout completed within roughly four hours, and most regions reported full server availability by the end of day one.

**Will my saved progress carry over after this HackHub community update?**

Yes, all progression, unlocks, and purchased items transfer automatically. The only exception is that any items duplicated through the now-fixed inventory glitch have been removed from flagged accounts to preserve fair play across the wider HackHub community update ecosystem.

**How long will the Phantom Protocol raid event run?**

The raid event lasts three weeks from the patch deployment date, which gives players plenty of time to clear all five stages even with a casual play schedule. Missed rewards may return in a future seasonal rotation, although the studio has not confirmed specifics yet.

**Do I need to restart my character after the balance changes?**

No character reset is required. The numerical changes to weapons and abilities apply retroactively, so your existing loadouts immediately benefit from any buffs like the Wraith's extended **Blink Strike** range introduced in this HackHub community update.

**Where can I report new bugs found after the patch?**

Players can submit bug reports through the official forum, the in-game feedback button, or the verified community Discord. The development team has committed to triaging new submissions weekly, which is faster than the monthly cadence reported during the previous season's HackHub community update cycle.
