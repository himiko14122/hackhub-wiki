The biggest change in the upcoming HackHub new update is a full mastery tree rework. Instead of the old linear perk ladder, players will allocate points across three independent branches: **Combat**, **Crafting**, and **Recon**. Each branch has 20 nodes, and cross-branch unlocks require spending points in both parents.

| Branch | Core Node | Effect | Cap Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Combat | Berserker | +12% melee damage | 3 ranks |
| Combat | Quickhands | -0.2s reload | 5 ranks |
| Crafting | Salvager | +8% scrap yield | 4 ranks |
| Crafting | Bulk Build | Queue 3 crafts | 1 rank |
| Recon | Pathfinder | +15% minimap range | 3 ranks |
| Recon | Sixth Sense | Highlight enemies 2s after combat | 2 ranks |

Resetting your tree costs a flat **1,500 credits** the first time per season, scaling up to 4,500 after the fourth reset. Players grinding the raid prep should plan their first allocation carefully, since resets during the launch window hurt more than later in the season.

### Two New Operative Classes

Two new classes are confirmed: **Cipher** (stealth support) and **Bastion** (tank anchor). Cipher deploys a **signal jammer** that disables enemy gadgets in a 12-meter radius for 4 seconds, while Bastion plants an **energy shield** absorbing 800 damage before collapsing.

| Class | Role | Signature Ability | Cooldown | Unlock Method |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cipher | Stealth Support | Signal Jammer | 18s | Reach Tier 25 in Battle Pass |

Community feedback on the test server indicates Bastion feels too defensive in solo queue, while Cipher is reportedly overperforming in coordinated 4-stacks.
