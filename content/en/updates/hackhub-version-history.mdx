### Balance Tuning Patches

Balance changes are the bread and butter of every HackHub version history entry. They rarely add anything new, but they reweight existing systems so that underused options become competitive again. According to community testing on the official beta branch, **balance patches are evaluated over a one-week window** before going live, which is why some nerfs feel delayed compared to the moment a strategy is first discovered by streamers. The flip side is that buffs often land a little hot because the developers err on the side of caution, and over-correction patches tend to follow two weeks later.

### New Content Drops

Content patches are the easiest to spot because they add named items, zones, or story chapters to the patch notes. These updates typically arrive in **three-month cycles**, which is the rhythm the developers have held since launch. Content drops also tend to bundle small balance changes, so always read the fine print before assuming a new item is the strongest option — sometimes the headline addition is a deliberate counter to whatever dominated the previous season.

### System Reworks

Reworks are the rarest and most disruptive entries in the HackHub version history. They rewrite a mechanic from the ground up, and old guides rarely survive them. The **v0.6 parry rework** and the **v1.2 zone rework** are the two clearest examples, since both reset years of accumulated community knowledge in a single patch. If you are returning to the game after a long break, reworks are the updates you must read carefully, because they invalidate more strategies than any other category combined.
