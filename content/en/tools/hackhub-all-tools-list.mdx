Offensive tools are the bread and butter of any HackHub build. They range from fast-firing sidearms to slow, high-damage heavy weapons, and each one fits a different combat scenario. ### Offensive Tool Categories

The game groups weapons into three broad archetypes, and every offensive entry on the HackHub tools list falls into one of them.

- **Light weapons** — fast fire rate, low per-shot damage, ideal for mob clearing and kiting.
- **Medium weapons** — balanced stats, the most versatile category and a safe starting pick for new players.
- **Heavy weapons** — slow fire rate, high damage, reserved for boss fights and elite encounters.

### When to Upgrade Offensive Tools

A common mistake among newcomers is upgrading every offensive tool the moment they unlock it. Community testing consistently shows that focusing upgrades on **one light and one heavy weapon** is more efficient than spreading resources across the entire arsenal, because each upgrade tier scales multiplicatively rather than additively. Pick a primary light weapon for daily farming and a primary heavy for boss content, then revisit other entries only after both are maxed.

## Tool Tiers and Rarity on the HackHub All Tools List

Every entry on the HackHub tools list carries a rarity tier that determines its base power ceiling and the materials needed to upgrade it. Understanding tiers helps you decide which items deserve your limited upgrade resources, especially when materials are scarce in the mid-game.

### Tool Rarity Tiers Explained

HackHub uses a five-tier rarity system that maps directly to upgrade difficulty and overall power. Each tier not only boosts raw stats but also unlocks unique mod slots that change how the tool behaves in combat.

- **Common (gray)** — starter tools, lowest stats, but free and easy to mass-produce.
- **Uncommon (green)** — slightly better stats, drops from standard missions.
- **Rare (blue)** — meaningful stat jumps, locked behind specific quest chains.
- **Epic (purple)** — endgame-grade, drops from challenging content.
- **Legendary (gold)** — best-in-slot, extremely rare, gated by endgame raids.

The table below summarizes how rarity impacts core attributes, based on aggregated community data rather than a single patch window.

|--------|----------------------|----------------------|------------------------|

A practical takeaway is that **Epic and Legendary tools** are not always strictly necessary for clearing all content, because some Rare weapons outperform Epic ones in niche scenarios after modding. Many players report completing the entire story and most side content using a well-upgraded **Rare** loadout, which is far cheaper to assemble than chasing purple drops. Save your legendary-tier materials for one or two favorite weapons rather than spreading them thin across every new drop you acquire.

### How to Get the Best Tools

If your goal is to own every entry on the HackHub all tools list, plan to spend time in endgame raids once the main campaign wraps. Legendary-tier drops are gated almost exclusively behind raid completions and weekly rotating events, while Epic items come from daily missions and tougher quest chains that require a coordinated group to clear efficiently. Joining an active clan or community Discord group dramatically shortens the grind because experienced players routinely trade materials and run group raids on schedule, which means you spend less time waiting for a full party and more time actually earning drops.
