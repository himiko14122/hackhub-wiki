## Understanding the Suspicion System in HackHub

Every move you make on a HackHub mission pushes the suspicion meter upward, and the game punishes careless play with escalating defensive responses. Sprinting near patrol routes, breaching a restricted server, or disabling a camera all add percentage points, and once the meter crosses a threshold, guards escalate behavior in ways that compound your risk for the rest of the run. Understanding how to reduce suspicion in HackHub starts with internalizing what each behavior costs before you ever load into a contract.

The meter is broken into four tiers, each triggering different responses from the AI network:

| Tier | Meter Level | Guard Response | Detection Radius |
|------|-------------|----------------|------------------|
| Calm | 0–24% | Standard patrol | 12 meters |
| Alert | 25–49% | Investigate last noise | 18 meters |
| Hostile | 50–74% | Active search patterns | 25 meters |
| Lockdown | 75–100% | Full reinforcements | All zones |

Each tier compounds your risk profile. A guard who heard footsteps at 30% suspicion will simply investigate the corner where the sound originated; the same guard at 60% suspicion will call for backup and tag your position to the entire network, painting every patrol in the mission with your silhouette. Community testing on the most recent patch confirms the detection radius scaling above, which means every percentage point saved early translates to significantly wider breathing room later in a run.

The HUD indicator sits in the upper-left corner of the screen, pulsing faster as the meter climbs. Subtle environmental cues like flickering overhead lights and slowed patrol rotations appear once the meter passes 40%, signaling that your approach is leaving too much evidence behind. Catching these cues early is the first skill every operator should develop, because by the time a guard shouts a detection call, the meter has already crossed a threshold you cannot reverse cheaply.

Before learning how to reduce suspicion, you need a clear picture of what generates it. HackHub tracks multiple noise and visibility vectors simultaneously, each weighted differently depending on the mission context. Beyond noise, line-of-sight determines how quickly the meter fills. Lighting zones also matter: bright server rooms double the fill rate, while shadow patches cut it in half. Players who master the crouch-and-hug-shadow rhythm routinely clear missions under 10% peak suspicion, which is why the top speedrun community treats posture as the single most important skill in the entire game.

## Advanced Stealth Strategies for Low Suspicion Runs

Once you've internalized the basics, the next layer of mastery involves sequencing your moves so the meter never climbs in the first place. The community calls this approach **ghost routing**, which means planning paths that naturally avoid every detection trigger before it happens rather than reacting after the fact.

### Route Planning Principles

Effective ghost routes share three traits: minimal patrol overlap, terminal access without line-of-sight exposure, and at least one panic room within 20 meters of any objective. Veteran operators map these by hand before each mission, marking guard patrol loops on paper and timing their rotations to the second. According to community data shared across Discord logs, runs planned this way average 18% lower peak suspicion than improvised attempts, and they finish roughly four minutes faster on average because the operator never has to wait out a triggered alert.

### Patrol Manipulation

Rather than avoiding patrols, advanced operators actively manipulate them. Throwing a coin into a side room pulls a guard away from their post for 12 seconds, opening a window for the primary objective. Pepper-spray grenades, which are craftable consumables, blind an entire squad for 8 seconds without adding suspicion, provided no one sees you throw them. Combine both tools and you can chain three-room clears with virtually zero meter gain, which is the benchmark that separates good operators from great ones.

### Timing Windows

Most HackHub missions feature scripted events that briefly stun all AI in a zone: power outages, false fire alarms, and EMP blasts from rival factions. These windows last between 5 and 15 seconds and are the only times sprinting is genuinely free. Track them on the mission timeline and save your boldest moves for those moments, because every second spent sprinting outside a timing window is a percentage point you will pay later.

## Common Mistakes That Spike Suspicion

Even experienced operators fall into habits that quietly inflate the meter without realizing it. The three biggest offenders show up in almost every uploaded replay that ends in a failed extraction.

- **Sprinting out of habit**: Sprinting adds 2% per second even when no one is watching, because the footstep audio registers regardless of AI line-of-sight. Walk by default, and sprint only inside confirmed timing windows.
- **Hacking terminals in lit rooms**: The 5%-per-second cost stacks with visibility penalties from bright lighting, doubling the effective meter gain during a hack. Move to a shadow tile before starting any terminal work.
- **Leaving bodies visible**: Neutralized guards must be dragged into a closet or vent within 10 seconds, or a patrol will find them and add 20% suspicion instantly, which is often the spike that triggers a lockdown cascade.

Fixing these three mistakes alone drops average peak suspicion by roughly 30%, based on community-reported before-and-after metrics shared across operator forums.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the fastest way to reduce suspicion in HackHub?**

The fastest single action is the Ghost Protocol ability, which instantly removes 15% suspicion at the cost of 40 energy. For sustained reduction, equip the Whisper Module in your chest slot to drain 1% every four seconds while stationary, and combine it with vent resets for compounded drops.

**Does crouching always help reduce suspicion level HackHub operators face?**

Crouching eliminates footstep audio and cuts line-of-sight detection to roughly one-sixth of standing values. It does not, however, block camera cones, which require a hard line-of-sight break regardless of posture. Always combine crouch movement with shadow positioning for maximum effect on the detection system.

**Can suspicion reach zero after a lockdown?**

Yes. Completing the primary objective during lockdown triggers a scripted extraction that resets the meter to zero as a reward mechanic. The mission still counts as a high-suspicion run on your statistics page, however, so aim to prevent lockouts through disciplined ghost routing rather than relying on late-stage resets to bail you out.

**Are there missions where low suspicion is impossible?**

A handful of story-mandatory missions force at least one detection event to advance the plot. Even there, your pre-event suspicion matters significantly: starting below 20% means the forced spike won't cascade into a full lockdown. Treat forced detections as budgeted costs rather than free passes, and the rest of the run stays clean.

**How does the suspicion meter interact with co-op play?**

In co-op, the meter is shared across the team, but individual mistakes only add to the shared pool rather than splitting it. A teammate who sprints through a patrol route hurts everyone equally. Coordinate movement speed and tool usage through voice chat to keep the shared meter low, applying solo discipline to the multiplayer context for the cleanest extractions.
