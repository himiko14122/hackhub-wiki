## How to Find and Verify Official HackHub Channels

Phishing is a real risk in any popular gaming community, and the HackHub player community is no exception. Fake servers clone the official branding, swap a few letters in the invite URL, and DM every new joiner with "free skin" scams. The community has responded with a clear verification checklist, and following it before you click any invite link saves you from stolen credentials.

### The Verification Checklist

Before joining any HackHub community Discord, run the search through the following filters:

1. **Check the official website footer** — the studio lists invite links in the sidebar with a verified timestamp.
2. **Cross-reference the member count** — legitimate servers show steady growth curves, not sudden spikes.
3. **Inspect the moderation team** — look for **rover-verified staff badges** next to admin names.
4. **Read the rules channel** — official servers publish a written code of conduct in the first 5 channels.
5. **Search for the studio's social media** — a verified Twitter/X or YouTube account will always link back to the Discord.

| Risk Signal | Likely Safe | Likely Scam |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Member count growth | +50–200/day | +10,000 in 24 hours |
| Staff verification | Rover badge visible | No badges at all |
| Primary rules channel | Pinned with date stamp | Empty or deleted |
| Invite link source | From official site footer | DM from random user |
| Bot behavior | Logs in #bot-status | DMs every new member |

A practical shortcut is to bookmark the verifier page inside the official HackHub developer forums, where the community maintains a curated list of safe invite links. If you find a server that isn't on that list, run the verification checklist yourself before posting any account information. For broader discovery, the HackHub community Discord also publishes a **monthly digest** every first Sunday, listing any newly sanctioned partner servers along with a brief description of their purpose.

## Etiquette and Tips for Engaging in HackHub Community Discord

Joining a HackHub community Discord is easy, but staying in good standing requires a few unwritten rules that the veteran members enforce through peer pressure and moderator action. ### Posting Best Practices

The HackHub community Discord runs on a culture of "search before you ask." Before posting a question, run a quick search in the channel's pinned messages and within the last 30 days of conversation. Nine times out of ten, your question has already been answered, and a quick reference will get you a faster response than a duplicate post. Players who spam the same question across multiple channels are routinely warned, and repeat offenders lose posting privileges for 24 hours.

### Building Reputation Over Time

The HackHub community Discord uses a **reputation system** where members earn XP through constructive posts, helpful answers, and consistent event participation. Higher tiers unlock perks like custom emoji slots, voice channel creation rights, and early access to community events. Players who invest 30 minutes a day for a month typically tier up from **Newcomer** to **Regular**, and that progression is what unlocks the most rewarding channels.

- **Newcomer (0–100 XP)** — read-only access to most channels
- **Regular (100–500 XP)** — full posting privileges everywhere
- **Trusted (500–2,000 XP)** — pin rights in `#help-desk` and access to `#dev-lounge`
- **Veteran (2,000+ XP)** — moderator-nominated, custom role creation

Veteran members often organize community-run tournaments that aren't advertised on the official channels. These smaller events are where the most competitive players sharpen their skills, and getting an invitation usually requires demonstrating helpfulness in the `#help-desk` and `#build-showcase` channels for several weeks. The HackHub community Discord rewards patience and contribution, and the best HackHub Discord culture reflects that investment back to the players who show up consistently.

If you're just starting out, spend your first week reading pinned messages, lurking in `#general-chat`, and answering one beginner question per day in `#help-desk`. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the official HackHub community Discord link?**

The official HackHub community Discord invite link is published in the footer of the game's official website and refreshed every six months. Avoid any invite sent via direct message, and always cross-check the link against the studio's verified social media before joining.

**How do I get verified on the HackHub Discord server?**

After joining, run the `!verify` command in any channel, link your in-game account through the OAuth popup, and confirm the captcha. The verification bot assigns the Verified role within 60 seconds, which unlocks posting in `#feedback`, `#bug-tracker`, and the role-selection channels.

**Are there regional HackHub Discord servers for non-English speakers?**

Yes, the HackHub community Discord operates an official hub for several regions, including Japan, Korea, Germany, France, Brazil, and Latin America. Each regional server runs in the local language and is moderated by community-elected native speakers who coordinate with the global staff team.

**Can I join multiple HackHub Discord servers at once?**

Absolutely. Many veteran players belong to the official server, a regional server, and at least one community-moderated build-sharing server. Discord lets you switch between servers from the sidebar, and you can mute individual servers to keep notifications clean.

**How do I report a scammer impersonating the HackHub developer team?**

Use the `!report` command in any verified HackHub Discord server, or DM the moderation team directly. Include screenshots of the impersonator's profile, the phishing message, and any invite link they shared. The moderation team will ban the account and post a warning in `#announcements` within 24 hours.
