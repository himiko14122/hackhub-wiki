## Understanding HackHub Nmap Tier Ranking Fundamentals

Before you slot a scanner into your deck, you need to grasp the core mechanics that define the **HackHub nmap tier ranking**. Unlike raw damage tools, scanners are entirely dependent on **probe delay timing** and **stealth thresholds**, which makes tier placement highly sensitive to your playstyle. A high-tier scanner in the hands of an aggressive **red-team runner** might perform worse than a mid-tier option used by a **ghost specialist** simply because of how detection mechanics work.

The ranking system evaluates scanners based on four primary attributes: **Packet Rate**, **Signature Depth**, **Trace Probability**, and **Bandwidth Cost**. These attributes don't exist in a vacuum because HackHub’s network simulation ties your scanner directly to the **Firewall Response Time** of the node you are attacking. If your probe delay is slower than the node's **counter-scan interval**, you will never map the full topology, which is why the community often debates S-Tier placements more than any other gear category.

### How Rating Factors Influence Tier Placement

The tier list community evaluates scanners using a weighted formula that prioritizes stealth over speed. Based on player reports and aggregated data, the breakdown looks like this:

| Rating Factor | Weight | Impact on Tier |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Trace Probability** | 30% | Lower values keep you below the **admin alert threshold** |

| **Bandwidth Cost** | 15% | Affects your ability to run concurrent scripts |

Players running high-risk **zero-day exploits** often need **Signature Depth** above all else, while those farming **data fragments** on low-security grids prioritize **Bandwidth Cost** to maximize uptime. The **HackHub nmap tier ranking** reflects these trade-offs, placing pure speed demons lower if they trigger immediate lockdowns.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best scanner in the HackHub nmap tier ranking?**

The **Quantum Whisper** is considered the best overall scanner because its **firewall inversion** mechanic allows you to bypass defenses that would normally require multiple specialized tools. However, it requires precise timing windows that can be punishing if you miss the activation window.

**Why does my scanner keep triggering admin alerts?**

This is almost always caused by using a scanner with a **Trace Probability** that exceeds the **stealth threshold** of your target node. You can mitigate this by upgrading your **stealth daemon** or switching to a scanner with a lower detection profile, like the **Silicon Ghost**.

**Are B-Tier scanners viable for late-game content?**

B-Tier scanners are generally not viable for late-game content because their **Signature Depth** is insufficient to resolve the complex **nested subnets** found in high-security grids. You will find yourself unable to detect critical **uplink nodes**, which will block your progression entirely.

**How often does the HackHub nmap tier ranking change?**

The **HackHub nmap tier ranking** is relatively stable during a patch cycle, but it can shift dramatically when new scanner modules are introduced or when balancing patches adjust **bandwidth costs**. The community usually updates their sheets within 48 hours of a major patch, according to player reports.

**What is probe staggering and how does it work?**

**Probe staggering** is an advanced technique where you manually interrupt your scanner at specific intervals before the full cycle completes, which reveals **partial topology leaks** and allows you to react to mid-scan discoveries without waiting for the full mapping to finish. It requires precise timing and a deep understanding of your scanner's **packet rate**.
